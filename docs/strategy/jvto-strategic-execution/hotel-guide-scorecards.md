# Hotel And Guide Scorecards

Measurable SLAs for accommodation and guide consistency. Consumes the product tiers from
`pace-product-architecture.md` and post-trip survey data; total review score alone is too
blunt, so guide, hotel, and access execution are scored separately.

## Hotel Scorecard

| Criterion | Weight | Scoring Guide |
|---|---:|---|
| Room cleanliness | 20 | 1 = unacceptable, 5 = consistently clean |
| Night noise | 20 | 1 = sleep frequently disrupted, 5 = quiet enough for early rest |
| Hot water and bathroom | 15 | 1 = unreliable, 5 = reliable |
| Bed comfort | 15 | 1 = poor rest, 5 = comfortable sleep |
| Early breakfast support | 10 | 1 = unavailable, 5 = reliable packed/early breakfast |
| Staff response | 10 | 1 = slow/unhelpful, 5 = fast resolution |
| Access and logistics | 10 | 1 = difficult for itinerary, 5 = smooth pickup/dropoff |

## Hotel Rules

- Minimum for Adventure Sprint: 70/100.
- Minimum for Balanced Explorer: 80/100.
- Minimum for Comfort Volcano Journey: 88/100.
- Two noise complaints within 30 days trigger inspection.
- Three complaints within 60 days pause the hotel for new bookings.
- Every route area must have one primary and one backup property.

## Guide Scorecard

| Criterion | Weight | Scoring Guide |
|---|---:|---|
| Safety briefing | 20 | Explains risks, equipment, health, and on-site authority rules |
| Punctuality | 15 | Pickup, departure, and handoff timing |
| Communication | 15 | Clear English/Bahasa and expectation setting |
| Guest care | 15 | Monitors fatigue, food, restroom, health, comfort |
| Storytelling | 10 | Explains place, culture, geology, local context |
| Photography support | 10 | Helps guests capture trip without delaying safety |
| Handling changes | 15 | Explains Plan B and keeps customer calm |

## Guide Rules

- Below 80/100 for two consecutive trips triggers coaching.
- Any missed safety briefing triggers same-week review.
- Any unlogged itinerary change triggers operations review.
- Any customer health incident requires a written incident report.

## Post-Trip Survey Questions

Rate 1-5:

1. The trip matched what was explained before payment.
2. The guide explained safety and daily expectations clearly.
3. The itinerary pace matched your ability and expectation.
4. You had enough rest for the activities you booked.
5. The hotel allowed you to rest properly before early departures.
6. Meal inclusions and own-cost meals were clear.
7. Any access change or Plan B was explained clearly.
8. The vehicle and driving experience felt safe.
9. Overall guide service.
10. Overall value for money.

Open questions:

1. What surprised you that should have been explained earlier?
2. Which hotel night was weakest?
3. Which part of the itinerary felt rushed?
4. Would you recommend this exact product to a friend with similar fitness?
